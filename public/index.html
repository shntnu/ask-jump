What's in this database?
Wells link plates to perturbations. Compound, CRISPR and ORF tables describe those perturbations. Control annotations identify DMSO and other controls. Plate metadata links to microscope settings and CellProfiler versions. General compound names, dose, treatment time and morphology measurements are absent.

  
Queries are read-only, limited to 200 returned rows and 8 seconds of execution. Each generation or review uses your Vercel AI Gateway credits. First use may take longer while the release database downloads.
